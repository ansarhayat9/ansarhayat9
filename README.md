## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧠 Recall AI — Assistive System for Alzheimer's & Dementia  `Final Year Project`
A multimodal AI companion for patients and caregivers.
- 🗣️ **RAG-based memory recall:** patients ask about past events by voice
- 👤 **Face recognition** and **real-time object detection** with audio feedback
- 📍 **GPS geo-fencing** with instant breach alerts
- 🩺 **Caregiver dashboard:** activity logs, medication reminders, emergency notifications

`Python` `Kotlin` `LangChain` `Whisper` `YOLO`

---

### 🏥 Hospital Appointment Voice Agent
Callers **schedule, cancel or list appointments** just by talking. Backed by a **FastAPI + SQLAlchemy/SQLite** service with a **Streamlit** demo dashboard.

`Python` `VAPI` `FastAPI` `SQLite` `Streamlit`

---

### 🧾 Invoice Processing Automation
A **no-touch digitization pipeline**: invoices dropped into Google Drive are OCR'd, parsed by an LLM into structured fields (vendor, dates, line items, tax, total), logged to a Google Sheets ledger and auto-archived. **Zero manual data entry.**

`n8n` `LlamaParse` `Groq (Llama 3.1)` `Google Drive` `Google Sheets`

---

### 🎯 Lead Research & Outreach Automation
Enter a **city + niche** → Apify scrapes Google Maps → leads are cleaned, **scored high / medium / low by an LLM**, and personalized outreach is generated for top prospects. **Hours of research reduced to minutes.**

`n8n` `Apify` `Groq (gpt-oss-120b)` `Google Sheets`

---

### 💬 RAG Conversational Chatbots
Multiple chatbots that answer from **custom document collections**, with ingestion, chunking, embeddings and **multi-turn memory**. Deployed through **WhatsApp and Slack**.

`Python` `LangChain` `OpenAI` `Vector DB` `n8n`
